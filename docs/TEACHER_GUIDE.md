# ShikshaSetu — Teacher Guide: Conducting Classroom Mode Without Student Phones

## Introduction

In many government and rural schools, students do not possess personal smartphones. **ShikshaSetu ensures no child is excluded due to lack of personal hardware.**

The platform offers two robust classroom workflows that require zero student smartphones:

---

## Workflow 1: Single-Device Classroom Projector Mode

Teachers can conduct the entire 3-day Water Conservation project using a single laptop, smartboard, or teacher tablet connected to a projector or TV screen.

### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **Log in:** Open the Teacher Portal and click **📺 कक्षा प्रोजेक्टर मोड (Classroom Projector Mode)**.
2. **Day 1 (Observe & Identify):**
   - Project Day 1 instructions onto the screen.
   - Read the guided questions aloud to the class.
   - Lead a class discussion on where water is used at home and in school.
   - Ask students to identify instances of leaks or overflowing water.
3. **Divide into Small Groups:**
   - Group 1 (गंगा), Group 2 (यमुना), Group 3 (सरस्वती), Group 4 (नर्मदा).
   - Each group discusses practical ways to stop water waste.
4. **Record Group Results On-Screen:**
   - Scroll to the **समूह सहभागिता दर्ज करें (Record Group Work)** section at the bottom of the screen.
   - Select the presenting group.
   - Type in the group's practical recommendations and click **समूह परिणाम दर्ज करें (Record Result)**.
   - The submission is immediately credited to the students and recorded in the class dashboard.

---

## Workflow 2: Printable Paper Observation Worksheets

For classrooms without continuous electricity or projectors:
1. Open **🖨️ प्रिंट कार्यपत्रक (Printable Worksheets)** in the Teacher Portal.
2. Select **दिवस 1**, **दिवस 2**, or **दिवस 3**.
3. Click **प्रिंट करें (Print / PDF)** to print copies on regular A4 paper.
4. Distribute the worksheets to students or student pairs.
5. Students fill in the observation boxes, tally tables, and draw the bar chart on paper.
6. The teacher reviews the physical paper, evaluates learning outcomes using the 3-tier rubric (Achieved / Developing / Needs Support), and enters the summary into the portal when convenient.
